How DeadlineIQ Works

A streamlined three-step workflow ensuring zero missed submissions across all departments.

01

Join Class Section

Register with your college email. Automatically synchronize with designated courses, assignments, and class announcements.

02

Smart Prioritization

Our dynamic engine calculates cutoff urgency. Instantly see what is Due Today, Due Soon, or Upcoming with color-coded chips.

03

Submit & Apply

Register for campus placement drives, submit laboratory assignments, and ask Gemini AI for guidance with a single click.
